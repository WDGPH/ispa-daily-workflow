# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful,
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible
or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that supports this community include:

- showing empathy and respect for different viewpoints and experiences,
- giving and accepting constructive feedback,
- taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them,
- focusing discussion on what is best for the project and affected operators,
- protecting student, client, school, and deployment privacy in every project
  space.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, threats, discriminatory language, or unwanted attention,
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, or personal attacks,
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission,
- sharing student records, credentials, operational details, or other sensitive
  material in public project spaces,
- conduct that maintainers reasonably consider inappropriate in a professional
  public-health collaboration.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in repository spaces and when an individual is
officially representing the project in public or private forums.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers or WDGPH GitHub
organization owners through an organization-approved channel. Do not include
student, client, credential, or other sensitive material in a report. If a
concern also involves security or privacy, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers will review reports promptly, fairly, and as confidentially as
practical. They may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening,
offensive, or harmful. Maintainers who have a conflict of interest in a report
should recuse themselves from its review.
